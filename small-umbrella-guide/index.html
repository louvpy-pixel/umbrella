Parts & Hardware

Check that all 6 parts, 8 long screws, 7 short screws, 4 hex nuts and the tools are included before assembly.

7 Easy Assembly Steps
1

Align the lower upright frame with the base frame. Start the screws loosely so the holes remain easy to align.

2

Fit the slotted middle shelf onto the lower section of the upright frame, with the raised lip facing forward.

3

Position the solid back panel above the shelf and align its side holes with the upright frame.

4

Place the upper guide frame over the back panel. Secure the corner holes with the long screws and hex nuts.

5

Secure both sides of the middle shelf and the center panel connections with the short screws.

6

Tighten the screws at both sides and the rear of the bottom frame. Check that the stand sits square and stable.

7

Place the removable white drip tray inside the bottom frame. Assembly is complete—check every screw once more before use.

Ready to use

Store long umbrellas, folding umbrellas and walking canes.

  • Place the stand on a flat, level floor before loading.
  • Empty and wipe the removable drip tray after wet-weather use.
  • Recheck the screws periodically and tighten if needed.
