15 Detailed Steps
1

Check the front and back direction before tightening. The smoother, flatter side is the front.

2

Make sure the protruding frame side faces the back/wall.

3

Place the base frame flat with brackets facing upward.

4

Use the long screws for the base brackets. The nuts are pre-installed in the frame; align first, then tighten.

5

Insert the side posts into the base brackets. Note the front and back sides.

6

Secure the lower joints with long screws and nuts.

7

Stand the frame upright and keep the base level. Note the front and back sides.

8

Secure the upper side holes with short screws on both sides.

9

Slide the shelf with holes onto the lower support.Make sure the shelf holes face the front.

10

Secure the shelf with short screws on both sides.

11

Insert the front umbrella holder frame straight down into the main stand.

12

Push the holder frame down firmly until it locks into place.

13

Hang the removable hooks through the front holes.

14

Press each hook fully into place before loading bags.

15

Place umbrellas in the stand and hang disposable umbrella bags as needed.

2 Ways To Use
A

Daily Storage

Place umbrellas directly into the stand for everyday use.

OR
B

Rainy Day Use

Hang disposable umbrella bags on the hooks first, then insert wet umbrellas.
